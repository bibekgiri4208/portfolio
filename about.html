My Story

My Story & Passions

A look into my creative background, physical setup configuration, and what inspires me outside of development.

Hi, I’m Bibek Giri, an IT student with a strong interest in design, technology, and creativity. Outside academics, I enjoy gaming, sketching, drawing, landscape and street photography, allowing me to balance my technical mindset with artistic expression.

I’m passionate about modern, minimalist aesthetics and maintain a clean, organized workspace with a single-monitor and laptop setup focused on functionality and productivity. I add personality through a growing collection of die-cast model cars, reflecting my interest in automotive design. Whether I’m studying, gaming, sketching, or creating, I enjoy environments where clean design, technology, and creativity come together.
