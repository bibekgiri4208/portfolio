Behind the Code

My Story & Passions

A look into my creative background, physical setup configuration, and what inspires me outside of development.

Hi, I'm Bibek Giri. I’m an IT student with a sharp eye for design and a passion for both digital and physical creativity. My academic life revolves around tech, but in my free time, I like to balance that logical mindset with my favorite hobbies: gaming and art. I’ve always been drawn to sketching and drawing, which allows me to express a more hands-on, artistic side outside of my screen time as well as my passion for landscape and street photography.

I’m a huge believer that your environment impacts your focus, so I’ve channeled my love for modern, minimalist aesthetics into creating a strictly clean and organized desk setup. My single-monitor and laptop workspace is built around keeping things clutter-free and functional. The only exceptions to the minimalism are a few curated details that reflect my personality—specifically, a growing collection of die-cast model cars, driven by a casual passion for automotive design. Ultimately, whether I'm diving into a game, sketching a new piece, or focusing on my studies, I thrive in spaces where clean design meets technology.
